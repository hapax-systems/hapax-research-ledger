## Steelman of the Original Disclosure (`LinkedIn, 2026-09-15`)

In its `2026-09-15` public product post addressing low-effort synthetic feed posts, LinkedIn avoided claiming that *"true AI-generated content fell by 40%"* unconditionally; instead, the text states the exact `15`-word clause *"Recent efforts have already helped reduce views of content classified as AI slop by 40%."* In production ranking workflows, tracking classifier-flagged impression counts (`C_t`) is standard operational telemetry, and general-audience product posts rarely append holdout confusion matrices or inter-rater reliability tables. Read strictly as a literal description of an internal classifier counter, the sentence is phrased accurately; the scientific identifiability gap arises when secondary commentary, industry benchmarks, or policy summaries cite *"LinkedIn cut AI slop by 40%"* as a verified causal reduction in true user exposure.

## Deterministic Identifiability & Witness Verification

### Witness 1: Coupling of True Prevalence, Classifier Operating Point, and Volume (`[S1]`, `[S2]`, `[S3]`)

Let `V_t` denote total feed impressions in period `t in {0, 1}`, `p_t in [0, 1]` denote the true share of impressions consisting of low-value synthetic content under a frozen human-rater rubric, `TPR_t = P(classified = 1 | true = 1)` denote sensitivity, and `FPR_t = P(classified = 1 | true = 0)` denote false-positive rate on non-slop posts. Expected views of content *classified* as slop satisfy (`[S1]`):

- `C_t = V_t * [p_t * TPR_t + (1 - p_t) * FPR_t]`

In general, the ratio `C_1 / C_0 = 0.60` (`-40.00%`) is a single scalar equation in seven coupled quantities (`V_1 / V_0, p_0, p_1, TPR_0, TPR_1, FPR_0, FPR_1`), or six parameters even if total feed impressions remain constant (`V_1 = V_0`). Publishing four compact methodological items from internal evaluation logs would resolve the mixture equation and permit re-evaluating `HIEH-2026-0004` toward `OUTCOME_1` or `OUTCOME_2`:
1. **Window & Denominator Specification (`t_0, t_1, V_1 / V_0`):** Calendar windows, holdout A/B control structure (if any), and whether `-40%` measures raw classified count `C_1 / C_0` or impression share `(C_1 / V_1) / (C_0 / V_0)`.
