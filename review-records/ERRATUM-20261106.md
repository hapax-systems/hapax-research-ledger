# Launch Slate 1: review-provenance erratum — 2026-11-06

Applies to `HIEH-2026-0001` through `HIEH-2026-0004` and `PUB-2026-0001`.

The earlier release-state and review wording overstate what the retained
provenance establishes. The review records contain `requested_model` identifiers
and `served_lineage` labels, but do not preserve sufficient call-level evidence
to establish endpoint-reported model identity independently of requested-name
fallback. The named-model review assurance is therefore **UNVERIFIED**.
This limitation does not establish substitution, fabricated identity, or that a
different model was served. It does not mean that model labels were absent or
that only requested identifiers were logged.

This notice changes review-provenance status. It does not validate or amend the
substantive findings, which remain under revision/review. **No new review** is
claimed. Prior quorum labels and gate results do not establish current acceptance;
any replacement or revised manuscript needs its own exact-byte review. Unchanged
witness arithmetic or a passing integrity check does not validate the science.
The earlier blanket mathematical/hygiene verification-complete assurance is not
renewed for these bytes. Sidecar privacy checklist items remain historical records;
the historical `PASS` is not a current verification or a current policy statement.

## What the retained records establish, and what they do not

The directory contains **13 JSON** files: ten manuscript-round records (five
manuscripts, two rounds each), two P6 summary files, and one gate receipt, alongside
the README. The ten manuscript-round files contain 30 `requested_model` fields
and 54 `served_lineage` fields; the summaries repeat 24 lineage labels. Across
the JSON records there are **54 unique response hashes**. These are file/field/hash
counts, not 54 independently attested model identities or a fresh count of calls.

Vendor fields and independently established endpoint-selection facts must be kept
separate from per-call model identity. Unattested model labels alone establish
neither model-family independence nor served-family exclusions. Historical
conflict-exclusion assertions remain available as assertions, not attestation.
This correction does not overturn independently established endpoint facts and
performs no new endpoint verification.

The later experiment collector's provenance is a separate evidence set from
these review calls. Experiment smoke-test identity observations cannot attest
review-call identity. Neither later collector code nor that experiment trace
establishes the executed version or fallback branch for any historical review
call. A bounded cache search is not evidence of global envelope absence.
